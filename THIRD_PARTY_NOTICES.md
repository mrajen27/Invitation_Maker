## Noto Sans Tamil

Vaanga Invite bundles Noto Sans Tamil from Google Fonts to render Tamil text in generated invitation images.

Engagement, naming ceremony, and baby shower photo-card backgrounds (`bg_engagement_*.webp`, `bg_naming_*.webp`, `bg_babyshower_*.webp`) are original illustrated artwork created for this app.
